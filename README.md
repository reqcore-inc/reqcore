# Reqcore

Reqcore is no longer maintained as an open-source project. Ongoing development has moved to a private codebase.

This was a difficult but practical decision. Maintaining a public, self-hosted edition alongside the hosted product was not financially sustainable for our small team. Focusing our limited time and resources on one product gives Reqcore the best chance to remain healthy and continue improving.

## What this means

- This repository is now a project-status notice; it does not contain the Reqcore source code.
- We are not accepting code contributions or publishing new open-source releases.
- Copies of Reqcore obtained from previous public releases remain governed by the licenses that accompanied them. This change does not take away rights already granted under those licenses.
- The hosted Reqcore product continues at [reqcore.com](https://reqcore.com).

We are sincerely grateful to everyone who tried Reqcore, self-hosted it, opened an issue, shared feedback, or contributed. The support and interest in the project have meant a great deal to us, and we understand that this change may be disappointing.

For questions about an existing Reqcore account, please contact [support@reqcore.com](mailto:support@reqcore.com).
